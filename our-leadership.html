Get to know the local leaders behind Chick-fil-A West Acres.

Sung Kim is the Owner/Operator of Chick-fil-A West Acres. He sets the restaurant’s direction and leads the team in serving the Fargo community with excellence.

Bio

Sung Kim was born in South Korea, grew up in Cambodia, and moved to the United States at 18. Shortly after graduating college, he began working at Chick-fil-A, and what started as a job soon became a passion. His greatest joy is getting to be a part of the mission, culture, and community and partnering with Chick-fil-A to serve others.

Beyond the restaurant, Sung serves as a Commissioned Officer in the U.S. Army Chaplain Corps as a reflection of his heart to serve the nation and invest in the next generation.

Owen Dieterle is the Back of House Director at Chick-fil-A West Acres. He leads the Back of House team to provide fast and accurate service and craveable food.

Owen grew up traveling the world as a child of missionary parents. In 2021, he graduated from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University in Daytona Beach, Florida, with a degree in Business Administration and Management.

He has worked with Chick-fil-A in high school and through college across two states and moved to Fargo in 2025 to continue his career with Chick-fil-A.

Bio

Anna Kempf is the Front of House Director at Chick-fil-A West Acres. She leads the Front of House team to provide fast and accurate service and a welcoming environment for guests.

Anna grew up in Minnesota and moved to Fargo in 2021 to attend North Dakota State University to pursue a degree in Emergency Management and Disaster Science.

She has worked at Chick-fil-A for six years with three different stores and four different operators. She is working towards her own application for Owner/Operator and has a genuine heart to see the good name of Chick-fil-A known in the Midwest.

Anna's favorite menu item is the seasonal Grilled Spicy Deluxe.
