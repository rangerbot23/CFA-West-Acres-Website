Bio

Anna Kempf is the Front of House Director at Chick-fil-A West Acres. She leads the Front of House team to provide fast and accurate service and a welcoming environment for guests.

Anna grew up in Minnesota and moved to Fargo in 2021 to attend North Dakota State University to pursue a degree in Emergency Management and Disaster Science.

She has worked at Chick-fil-A for six years with three stores and four operators under her belt. She is working towards her own application for Owner/Operator and has a genuine heart to see the good name of Chick-fil-A known in the Midwest.

Anna's favorite menu item is the seasonal Grilled Spicy Deluxe.
