Policy category 01

Scheduling Policies

Review these guidelines before submitting a request or changing your availability.

Time off requests

Request time off

Time off requests must be submitted two weeks in advance through HotSchedules.

Availability change requests

Change availability

Availability can only be changed four times a year.

Youth employment

Hour restrictions

Workers ages 14–15 need a Youth Employment Certificate. Outside school hours, they may work:

  • School days: up to 3 hours per day; school weeks: up to 18 hours.
  • Non-school days: up to 8 hours per day; non-school weeks: up to 40 hours.
  • Labor Day through May 31: 7 a.m.–7 p.m.
  • June 1 through Labor Day: 7 a.m.–9 p.m.

Read North Dakota’s full youth employment rules ↗

Shift coverage

Getting your shift covered

You must release all or part of your shift through HotSchedules, another person must pick it up, and the shift leader(s) in charge during the time of your shift must approve it. You must get approval for shifts you are trying to release; otherwise, you are still responsible for the shift and may face disciplinary action if you are not present.
