Our founder

S. Truett Cathy

A story shaped by faith, family, hospitality, and care for people.

01 / THE BEGINNING

A small diner with a big idea

In 1946, S. Truett Cathy and his brother Ben opened the Dwarf Grill in Hapeville, Georgia. The first Chick-fil-A restaurant opened in Atlanta’s Greenbriar Mall in 1967.

02 / FAMILY

A life centered on family

Family was central to Cathy’s life and work. Chick-fil-A remains a family-owned business.

03 / CUSTOMERS

A deep love for customers

He invited customers to taste early versions of the chicken sandwich and share feedback as he refined the recipe.

04 / TEAM MEMBERS

A heart for employees

He believed in helping people learn, grow, and find opportunities through their work.
