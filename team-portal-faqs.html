Quick answers

All FAQs

HR

How do I set up my direct deposit?

Matt LaForce-Luster can help you in setting up your direct deposit. Please have your bank name, account type, routing number, account number, and amount or percentage you wish to deposit ready.

Where can I find my paystub?

Ask Matt LaForce-Luster. Be prepared to have date range for requested pay period ready.

When can I get my check if I don't have direct deposit?

On Thursdays of pay weeks, a Front of House Shift Leader can give you your check.

How can I check the status of my training?

Reach out to your respective assistant director for an update on your training.

Scheduling

When is the schedule posted?

The schedule for the following week is posted weekly on Thursdays.

How do I submit an availability change request?

See the scheduling policies for details. Assistant Shift Leaders can show you how to submit these requests in HotSchedules.

How do I submit a time off request?

See the scheduling policies for details. Assistant Shift Leaders can show you how to submit these requests in HotSchedules.

How do I get my shift covered?

See the scheduling policies for details. Then reach out to the Shift Leader(s) leading during the time of your shift for approval of the swap/release.

What is considered an unexpected emergency?

Death of a family member, hospitalization of you or an immediate family member, or traffic accident.

Who can answer scheduling questions not answered here?

For Front of House scheduling questions, contact Anna Kempf. For Back of House, contact Owen Dieterle. Please review the scheduling policies before reaching out.

General

How do I get access to a platform?

Access to HotSchedules, OneClick, or Discord requires your respective assistant director. An assistant shift leader can grant Chick-fil-A Portal access.

Where can I find Links, Applications, and team events?

Use the Links, Applications, and Calendar buttons on the Team Portal landing page to open those team pages.
